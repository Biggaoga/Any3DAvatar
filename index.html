Overview

Why Any3DAvatar?

Prior single-image head reconstruction methods face two coupled bottlenecks: they either compromise reconstruction quality or require an expensive processing pipeline to obtain high-quality results.

01

Quality bottleneck

Incomplete and inconsistent full-head reconstruction

  • Fragile details around teeth, hair, and accessories
  • Incomplete back-of-head geometry and appearance
  • Identity and texture degradation across novel views
02

Speed bottleneck

High-quality outputs depend on costly processing

  • Heavy preprocessing before reconstruction
  • Multi-stage pipelines with accumulated overhead
  • Per-subject optimization repeated for each identity
Our response

Any3DAvatar addresses both bottlenecks with a single-stage 3D Gaussian framework: few-step conditional denoising produces a complete, high-fidelity head directly from one portrait, with inference as fast as 0.64 seconds.

01

Structured 3D generation

Plücker-conditioned multi-view noise-token initialization provides a structured starting point for full-head reconstruction.

02

Appearance supervision

A view-conditioned image branch supervises shared latent tokens without adding inference-time computation.

03

Diverse training data

AnyHead combines identity coverage, dense viewpoints, complete geometry, and realistic accessories.

Dataset

AnyHead data suite

AnyHead combines diverse identities, dense multi-view supervision, complete-head geometry, and accessory-rich samples to support robust full-head reconstruction.

Method

A direct path from portrait to 3D Gaussians

Image context and a structured Gaussian scaffold are jointly processed by a DiT backbone, then decoded into a renderable full-head avatar.

  1. 1

    Tokenize the observation

    Encode the portrait with Plücker rays and initialize a structured view-conditioned Gaussian scaffold.

  2. 2

    Joint conditional denoising

    Process context and Gaussian tokens together through the shared DiT backbone for a small number of steps.

  3. 3

    Decode geometry and appearance

    Produce renderable 3D Gaussians while the auxiliary image branch strengthens novel-view texture learning.
